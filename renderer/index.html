Nutrition data source

Food search always checks a built-in catalog of common home-style Indian dishes (dal, roti, biryani, idli, sambar, etc.) plus Open Food Facts — a free, global, crowd-sourced product database with good coverage of Indian brands (Amul, Britannia, Haldiram's, MTR, Parle, and more). No API key needed for either.

When set, search tries Gemini first (with Google Search grounding, so answers cite live results rather than the model's memory) before falling back to Open Food Facts — useful for dishes and regional/branded items OFF doesn't carry. Gemini automatically cascades across several model tiers as each one's free daily quota runs out. Results from Gemini are marked AI ESTIMATE in search — review them, since even a grounded model can get a number wrong, unlike Open Food Facts' sourced product data.
